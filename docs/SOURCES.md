# Sources, assumptions and provenance

Reviewed on 11 November 2026. Source availability does not establish applicability to this notional vehicle.

1. **B.N. Suresh and K. Sivan (2015), Integrated Design for Space Transportation System, Springer.** DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-81-322-2532-4. The user supplied this book. Its methods and actual chapter structure organize the workflow. Chapters 3–5 cover orbital/staging fundamentals, 6 environment, 7–8 requirements/integration, 9 propulsion, 10 aerodynamics, 11 structures, 12 thermal, 13 auxiliary systems and 14 NGC. The full copyrighted book is not redistributed in this code package.
2. **SpaceX, Falcon User's Guide, May 2025.** https://www.spacex.com/assets/media/falcon-users-guide-2025-05-09.pdf. Supports Merlin 1D 845 kN sea-level thrust, throttle range and the distinction between standard M1D and MVac. The guide describes Falcon's own configuration; TARA_LV's two-standard-M1D upper stage is a new notional integration. The inherited 282/311 s Isp, engine mass, O/F and assumed nozzle/gimbal dimensions are **not established by this guide** and remain provisional inputs.
3. **ISRO, Successful completion of Human rating of CE20 Cryogenic engine for the Gaganyaan Programme, 21 February 2024.** https://www.isro.gov.in/Successful_completion_Human_rating_CE20_Cryogenic_engine.html. Describes CE20 testing and 19–22 tonne thrust capability with 442.5 s Isp. The selected 22 tonne-force value is converted using standard g0. `CE32` remains a project label. That ISRO qualification does not qualify TARA_LV's vehicle integration.
4. **NASA-STD-5001B with Change 3, 24 October 2022.** https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-5001. Structural design/test-factor framework. https://standards.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/standards/NASA/B-w/CHANGE-3/3/2022-10-24-NASA-STD-5001B-w-Change-3-Approved.pdf. The selected metallic yield/ultimate factors are screening choices with applicability requiring project review; pressure systems, composites, joints, glass, fracture control and test philosophy require separate treatment.
5. **NASA-STD-8719.29, NASA Technical Requirements for Human-Rating, baseline 11 December 2023.** https://standards.nasa.gov/node/6964. Covers human-flight safety/control/failure tolerance/proximity/crew-survival topics. Used to identify open assurance work; no numerical compliance claim is made. Older NPR 8705.2 revisions were not used as the current technical standard.
6. **User-supplied BAS_2D_Research archive.** Inherited launch-site coordinates, mission seeds, standard-Merlin 282/311 s assumptions and the conservative Euler, Timoshenko beam and delay-aware POGO kernels were reviewed. Those kernels retain their original MIT license notice through the project LICENSE. The obsolete MATLAB MVac/6.5 m deck and stale trajectories are not executable dependencies.
7. **The two user-supplied alternative-mission README PDFs.** Used for the requested presentation/output categories only. Their Venus-mission vehicle, strap-on, engine, numerical results and unrelated chapter/equation numbering were not imported.

## Parameters needing authoritative replacement

The selected structural-factor bounds, welded-metal allowables, engine mass/dimensions/O-F, minimum T/W, damping, pressure spectra, POGO transfer functions, wind profile, base heating, foam/TPS properties, baffle properties, secondary hardware allowances and illustrative failure probabilities are explicit design assumptions. They are not represented as measured or supplier-qualified data.

The staging optimum is conditional on those assumptions. The geometry is not a build drawing. Numerical checks verify selected equations and conservation; wind tunnel, engine, structural, thermal and integrated flight testing are required for physical validation and human-flight qualification.
